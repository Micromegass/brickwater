# Surface: home (app/(de)/page.tsx, app/(en)/en/page.tsx)

Scope: the front door for fans and bookers. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience and job: a fan or booker wants the sound, the record, the collective, the next show and the booking address, in that order.
Action: primary "Konzerte"; secondary "Musik hören" and an Instagram "Folgen" sticker; the hero label plays the song he leads with. Booking email closes the page.
Proof/content: the artist's own words, his photographs, the three videos he chose, four releases with lyrics, two human-made artworks.
Constraints: no fabricated proof, no tracking, light scene, no WebGL, nothing that shouts.

Direction: the site is the record package, in the sleeve's own colours. Every section sits on paper a pigment soaked into, with two or three of the artist's brush marks drifting behind it on slow wave paths.
First viewport: one frame. The wordmark filled with the sleeve's wash and set flush with the tagline, three actions, the show badge and the record label that plays one song on the left, the harmonica photograph on the right in plain black and white, at its own proportion, with the site's usual print corner and shadow, five marks billowing behind all of it and the wolves gathering out of them. It fills the screen under the header from md up.
Memorable moments: the paint soaking into the wordmark once on load, the background breathing until the two wolves gather in it and come apart again, a song playing from the hero label, a song opening its lyrics in a panel in that record's colour, and a photograph opening full height over the page.
Scale: quiet. Display tops out at 3.25rem, headings at weight 600, measure 1160px.
Section order (the artist's, asserted by an end-to-end test): hero, bio and photographs, videos, music, collective, shows, booking. The photo strip links on into the gallery, which holds all nineteen photographs.
Unresolved: no upcoming shows at launch, so the badge says "folgt bald" and still links to the shows page.
