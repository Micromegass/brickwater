# Surface: home (app/(de)/page.tsx, app/(en)/en/page.tsx)

Scope: the front door for fans and bookers. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience and job: a fan or booker wants the sound, the record, the collective, the next show and the booking address, in that order.
Action: primary "Konzerte"; secondary "Musik hören". Booking email closes the page.
Proof/content: the artist's own words, his photographs, the three videos he chose, four releases with lyrics, two human-made artworks (the Season One watercolour, the Folk's Worst Nightmare ink drawing).
Constraints: no fabricated proof, no tracking, one authored motion moment, light scene, no WebGL, nothing that shouts.

Direction: the site is the record package, in the sleeve's own colours. Jacket front = the wordmark filled with a wash mixed from the Season One painting; insert = videos; inner sleeve = releases and lyrics on paper the sage soaked into; the collective's own sleeve and photograph on clay-soaked paper; back cover = shows; sticker sheet = socials; runout etching = footer.
Memorable moment: the paint soaks into BRICKWATER once on load, then the page holds still.
Scale: quiet. Display tops out at 3.25rem, headings at weight 600, measure 1160px. Presence comes from space and pigment, never from size.
Manchas: one or two of the artist's brush marks per section, inside the section, behind the content, 0.12 to 0.26 opacity.
Section order (the artist's, asserted by an end-to-end test): hero, bio and photographs, videos, music, collective, shows, booking.
Unresolved: no upcoming shows at launch, so the hype sticker carries the latest release and the shows section states the truth plainly.
